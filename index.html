About Financial Literacy

Overview of Monitoring, Evaluation, and Implementation under NS2.0 framework.

Overview

Financial literacy focuses on improving knowledge, behaviour, and decision-making skills related to personal finance through structured national programmes.

Main Objectives

To improve financial awareness, encourage responsible spending, increase savings behaviour, and strengthen financial resilience among Malaysians.

Monitoring & Evaluation

Uses frameworks such as FCI Survey and FEME Framework to measure impact, track progress, and improve programme effectiveness.

Implementation Timeline

2026 – Planning and national awareness programmes
2027 – Expansion of financial education initiatives
2028 – Mid-term evaluation and assessment
2029 – Improvement and policy refinement phase
2030 – Final evaluation and impact reporting

Expected Outcome

A financially literate society with better financial planning skills, improved savings habits, and stronger long-term financial stability.

Main Information

Important topics discussed in Financial Literacy.

Main Goals

The Monitoring and Evaluation Framework aims to track the success of NS2.0 initiatives while improving financial literacy programmes nationwide.

FCI Survey

The Financial Capability and Inclusion Demand Side (FCI) Survey measures Malaysians’ financial behaviour, knowledge, and attitudes.

FEME Framework

The Financial Education Measurement and Evaluation Framework evaluates programme effectiveness and financial well-being outcomes.

Stakeholders

Government agencies, organisations, and financial institutions collaborate to strengthen financial literacy initiatives.

Macro-Level Monitoring

Focuses on headline indicators such as financial resilience, digital financial literacy, debt management, and savings behaviour.

Micro-Level Monitoring

Tracks delivery plans, stakeholder responsibilities, timelines, and implementation progress for each initiative.

Annual Reviews

Annual reviews monitor the effectiveness of strategies while identifying improvements needed for future programmes.

Financial Resilience

NS2.0 aims to improve financial resilience among Malaysians through better planning, savings, and informed financial decisions.
